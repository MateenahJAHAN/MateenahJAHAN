# Mateenah Jahan

I’m a predoctoral research fellow through the Fatima Fellowship. I work with Dr. Marouen Ben Guebila to study how drug-induced changes in gene expression relate to sex-associated patterns across human tissues. I also work on T-cell repertoire modelling and evaluate how language models behave when they use tools. [Dana-Farber lab team](https://labs.dana-farber.org/viswanathanlab/our-team)

[Research portfolio](https://mateenahjahan.github.io/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mateenah-jahan/)

## Research and code

- **Sex Differences in Drug-Induced Gene Expression.** My fellowship project compares CMap drug responses with GTEx tissue patterns and examines how cell lineage and sampling affect the comparison. [Exploratory CMap / GTEx notebook](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/Project-59-CMAP-Sex-Differences/blob/main/Phase6_NoSampleFilter/notebook/phase6_no_sample_filter.ipynb)
- **CAR-T treatment response.** At CSIR-IGIB, I analysed 239,637 T-cell receptor sequences from 34 patients using DeepTCR. I also contributed to the framework’s Python 3.10 and TensorFlow 2.12 update. [Project code and report](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/Final_Deliverable_DeepTCRResearch_Mateenah_Jahan_CSIR) · [Nature Communications paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21879-w) · [Upstream acknowledgement](https://github.com/sidhomj/DeepTCR/commit/715f6642292a296aa22ffba1b219908fd5af5c5f)
- **Biomni-AD.** I used Biomni agents to generate and prioritise Alzheimer’s drug repurposing hypotheses. I was one of three Round 1 winners of the ADA Consortium’s Biomni-AD Discovery Prize. [Workflow and results](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/biomni-ad-drug-repurposing) · [Prize announcement](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/MateenahJAHAN.github.io/blob/main/assets/biomni-ad-winners.png)
- **MedGemma evaluation.** I published 13 probes with model responses and reference answers for hematologic oncology and control tasks. [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mateenah/medgemma-4b-hematologic-oncology-blind-spots)
- **TARA tool-calling study.** In TARA’s Bengaluru cohort, part of its first round in India, I studied mechanistic interpretability through the ARENA-based curriculum. My project tested when Qwen attempts a tool call or abstains across two interfaces. It includes 224 held-out generations, linear probes and a steering intervention that failed to transfer. [TARA curriculum](https://www.taraprogram.org/curriculum) · [Code, data and results](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/tara-chat-tool-study)
