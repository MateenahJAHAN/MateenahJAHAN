# Mateenah Jahan

I’m a Fatima Fellow in the fellowship’s 2026 cycle. I work with [Dr. Marouen Ben Guebila](https://labs.dana-farber.org/viswanathanlab/our-team) of Dana-Farber Cancer Institute to study how drug-induced changes in gene expression relate to sex-associated patterns across human tissues. I also work on T-cell repertoire modeling and evaluate how language models behave when they use tools.

[Research portfolio](https://mateenahjahan.github.io/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mateenah-jahan/)

## Research and code

- **Sex Differences in Drug-Induced Gene Expression.** My fellowship project compares CMap drug responses with GTEx tissue patterns and examines how cell lineage and sampling affect the comparison. It led to a draft manuscript with Dr. Ben Guebila on whether the donor-sex labels of cancer cell lines match what the cells express. [Draft manuscript](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/Project-59-CMAP-Sex-Differences/blob/main/manuscript/manuscript.pdf) · [Exploratory CMap / GTEx notebook](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/Project-59-CMAP-Sex-Differences/blob/main/Phase6_NoSampleFilter/notebook/phase6_no_sample_filter.ipynb)
- **CAR-T treatment response.** At CSIR-IGIB, I analyzed 239,637 T-cell receptor sequences from 34 patients using DeepTCR. With whole patients held out, the model did not reliably predict response. I also contributed to the framework’s Python 3.10 and TensorFlow 2.12 update. [Project code and report](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/Final_Deliverable_DeepTCRResearch_Mateenah_Jahan_CSIR) · [Nature Communications paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21879-w) · [Upstream acknowledgement](https://github.com/sidhomj/DeepTCR/commit/715f6642292a296aa22ffba1b219908fd5af5c5f)
- **Biomni-AD.** I used Biomni agents to generate and prioritize Alzheimer’s drug repurposing hypotheses. I was one of three Round 1 winners of the ADA Consortium’s Biomni-AD Discovery Prize. [Workflow and results](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/biomni-ad-drug-repurposing) · [Prize announcement](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/MateenahJAHAN.github.io/blob/main/assets/biomni-ad-winners.png)
- **MedGemma evaluation.** I published 13 prompts with model responses and reference answers for hematologic oncology and control tasks. [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mateenah/medgemma-4b-hematologic-oncology-blind-spots)
- **TARA tool-calling study.** In TARA’s Round 2 Bengaluru cohort, I studied mechanistic interpretability through the ARENA-based curriculum. My project tested when Qwen attempts a tool call or abstains across two interfaces. On paired requests, two small Qwen models called tools they did not need more often through the native tool interface than through a plain-text tool list. [TARA curriculum](https://www.taraprogram.org/curriculum) · [Code, data and results](https://github.com/MateenahJAHAN/tara-chat-tool-study)
